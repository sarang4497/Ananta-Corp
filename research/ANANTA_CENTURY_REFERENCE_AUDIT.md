## 2. Extracted principles (for Ananta)

1. **Desktop header: two rows, ~130–140px, sticky.** Logo spans both rows on the left (~180px wide).
   - Row 1 (top right): primary trust/offer pill, search field (~220px), then icons.
   - Row 2: main menu, then WhatsApp icon, then a "Call us" block with the label on top and the number in bold.
   - When scrolled it only tightens slightly (~10–15px). Keep it at about 110–120px for Ananta.
2. **Mobile header: two stacked bars, ~115px in total.**
   - Bar 1 (~50px, brand colour): search icon, WhatsApp icon, tap-to-call number.
   - Bar 2 (~65px, white): logo on the left; on the right, an Enquiry/quote icon, then the hamburger.
   - Ananta does not need the mic, wishlist or cart.
3. **Keep the top-level menu to 6 or fewer items, each with a chevron.** Suggested for Ananta: Products · Brands · Tools · Inspiration · Dealers/Contact · About. Show the active or hovered item in brand colour with the chevron flipped.
4. **Products mega menu: full-width, ~6 columns.** Bold category heading per column, 3–6 sub-links with thin dividers, and "More" as the last column. For Ananta: Plywood | MDF | HDHMR | Laminates | Doors | Smart Locks.
5. **Small menus use a "links + image" panel.** About 40% of the panel is a link list and 60% is an image. Use this for 2–4-item menus such as Inspiration, Services and Contact.
6. **Tools menu: 2 link columns, a 2–3-line promo block and one image.** Grouping all calculators and visualizers under one "Tools" item makes them easy to find.
7. **Fixed right-edge FAB rail, ~44×50px per button, about 60% down the viewport.** Three stacked buttons: Enquire (form icon) → Call → WhatsApp (green). Show it on every page, both desktop and mobile. A narrow rail is less intrusive than a full bottom bar.
8. **Enquiry modal: one column, ~480px wide, red close button sitting on the corner.**
   - Fields: Name, Phone, Email, State + City (side by side), Product (prefilled when the modal is opened from a product card), Message, consent checkbox.
   - Buttons: full-width primary "Send Enquiry", then a row with "Chat on WhatsApp" and "Call" next to each other.
   - For Ananta, make Email optional and replace State/City with a single City/Area field.
9. **Open the enquiry modal on click only.** Century auto-opens it on load. Don't copy that; trigger it from CTAs and the FAB.
10. **Audience routing.** Offer a switcher in the utility bar with a 2×2 chooser (icon + Explore), not a page-load modal. Ananta's audiences: Homeowner / Architect & Interior Designer / Contractor-Carpenter / Dealer-Bulk. Each choice routes to a tailored landing page.
11. **Category listing: one horizontal card per product.**
    - Layout: image ~35%, info panel ~65%.
    - Info panel order: tag pills (Waterproof / BWP / grade) → brand or series logo → 2–3-line description → feature chips (icon + 1–2 words, 4–5 in a row with arrows) → coloured CTA strip with a ghost "Know More" and a solid "Enquire Now".
    - Mobile stacks the same order vertically.
12. **"Find Product" filter above long lists on mobile.** Use a full-width primary button that opens filters (thickness, grade, brand, application).
13. **Tabbed "Key USPs" block below the listing.** One pill tab per product with a caret under the active tab. The panel shows 4 large icons (~80px) with 1-line captions. Mobile shows 2 per view with arrows. Good for comparing grades without a table.
14. **Section headings use one consistent pattern.** A large light H2 (~44px), then a letter-spaced uppercase eyebrow in brand colour (~16px), then an optional 1–2-line intro. Carousel arrows (dashed circles) and "View All" sit top right.
15. **Home page section order.** Hero carousel → shop-by-room icon row (5 tiles) → Tools tab bar with preview → featured products (4-up) → expert-advice persona cards → blog/inspiration carousel (3-up) → testimonials (3-up) → FAQ → long SEO "About" text with Read More → footer.
16. **FAQ layout.** On desktop, image or illustration on the left (~30%) and accordion on the right (~65%), with the first item open. Use brand-coloured +/− at the right edge and hairline dividers. Show 3–6 items per page and mark them up with FAQ schema. Repeat FAQs on category and tool pages.
17. **Requirement calculator pattern.**
    - Hero with a question headline ("How many sheets do you need?").
    - Overlapping white card with step 1: round icon picker (wardrobe, bed, kitchen, door, shelving, table, sofa, panelling).
    - Step 2: variant dropdown.
    - Result: thumbnail rail + render + a table of sheet count by thickness, with a ft/cm toggle.
    - Add an Ananta improvement: an "Enquire for this quantity" CTA that sends the table into the enquiry form.
    - Put SEO H2 content below the tool.
18. **Breadcrumbs** go in a thin full-width grey bar under the header, right-aligned on desktop, on every inner page.
19. **Dealer/partner form.** On desktop use a 2-column grid with State/City/District as a 3-up row. On mobile, collapse to 1 column (Century keeps 2–3 columns, which squashes the fields). Follow the form with a partner gallery and testimonials that include the company name.
20. **Trust stack on B2B pages.** Awards carousel → certificates carousel grouped by brand, then product tab, then logos → partner testimonials. For Ananta, show the authorised-distributor badge from each brand instead.
21. **Footer.**
    - Desktop, dark background: column 1 (~35%) has logo, a 3–4-line blurb, Read more and social icons; then 4 link columns (Products / Tools / Inspiration / Company).
    - Phone number top right; outlined "Dealer Locator / Visit Showroom" and "Customer Support" buttons at the bottom right.
    - Legal links on a separate light strip.
    - Mobile collapses the columns. Use accordions here, because Century drops the links entirely.
22. **Mobile drawer.** Opens from the left at ~75% width. Header row has "Login" or the WhatsApp/phone and a close button. Top-level items are accordions with circular +/− buttons; the open item turns brand colour and its sub-links get a ↗ icon. Pin a primary CTA ("Get a Quote") at the bottom of the drawer.
23. **Search UX.** On desktop, an always-visible inline field in the header with placeholder "What are you looking for?". On mobile, a search icon that opens a full-screen overlay. Add suggestions for categories, brands and thicknesses.
24. **Product cards on the home page:** 4-up on a light grey band, with the packshot and an inset lifestyle thumbnail. Century also shows a QR code on each card; Ananta should drop it (see section 3).
25. **Mobile bottom navigation.** Century has none; the bottom bar in the phone shots is Safari's own toolbar. For Ananta's trade audience, consider either a 4-item bottom bar (Products · Calculator · WhatsApp · Enquire) or keep the FAB rail. Don't run both.

## 3. Do not copy

- Any Century names, sub-brands, technology names (Firewall, ViroKill), warranty programmes, test counts, certifications, awards, prices, taglines or testimonials.
- Century's red/white colours, condensed display font, star logo, and the red pill at the top of the header.
- The enquiry modal opening automatically on page load. Combined with the persona modal, two modals stack on first visit.
- Wishlist, cart, "Shop Now" and voice-search mic. These e-commerce features don't fit a distributor taking enquiries.
- QR codes on product cards and listing pages. They add visual noise and do nothing on screen.
- Duplicate header icons. WhatsApp and phone appear in the header, the FAB rail and the modal at the same time. Choose one place per surface.
- 2–3-column form layouts on mobile, where fields get truncated (e.g. "Alternative Business Nam").
- The mobile footer that drops every link column.
- Long keyword-heavy "best plywood in India" SEO text blocks. Write short, factual, Surat-local copy instead.
- Feature-chip carousels that hide chips behind arrows on desktop, cutting labels off mid-word. If there are 4–5 chips, show them all.
- Mixed CTAs (Enquire Now / Add To Cart) across cards in the same list. Ananta should use one consistent primary CTA.
