Researched 2026-11-02 (read-only). We take **patterns** from these sites, never their claims, numbers, specs,
warranties or copy.

## Principles adopted for Ananta

1. **Browse by material first, then by grade or use.** The mega menu and material library are organised as
   category → grade (Plywood → MR / BWP; MDF → Interior / Exterior / HDHMR …). That follows Greenpanel's clarity.
2. **A second route in by application** (kitchen, wardrobe, wet zones, doors, office furniture), like Greenply and
   CenturyPly. It is implemented as the application filter and the material selector, mapped only to A1 copy.
3. **A "which grade do I need" helper**: the deterministic Material Selector, built on Ananta's own FAQ answers
   (Duroply's pattern).
4. **One consistent product-page layout**: what it is → specification table → best for → standards attributed to
   the manufacturer → FAQ → enquire. Specs are written as text in rows, never inside images.
5. **Name the source of technical claims** (the attribution line says, for example, "as stated by Action TESA"),
   and state limits ("covered, non-wet exposure").
6. **An enquiry action on every product and décor, with the product name prefilled** (WhatsApp prefill + the
   contact form's product field).
7. **WhatsApp + call always reachable on mobile**, but as one coordinated contact bar, not stacked bubbles.
8. **Make finishes browsable**: a swatch wall with code + name and filters by family (solid / wood / fabric /
   stone / texture).
9. **A small, complete menu beats a big, half-built one.** Every Ananta menu item leads to a real page or anchor.
   There is no "find a designer", no calculator and no downloads section, because Ananta has no catalogue PDFs to
   put in it.
10. **Trust signals only when documented**: real brand logos, the year, the address and GSTIN, and real customer
    quotes. There are no dealer badges, warranty seals or certification logos at company level.

Patterns we deliberately **did not** adopt: a sheet calculator (it needs yield assumptions we cannot verify), a
dealer locator (Ananta is a single location) and a downloads page (no owner documents exist).
