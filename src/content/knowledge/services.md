# Services

We offer 7 core services. Each has a dedicated page — use the exact URL when pointing someone there. The throughline across all of them: we don't just advise or run campaigns, we BUILD and run the systems, AI-assisted where it adds leverage.

## Lead Generation — /lead-generation
"Qualified demand, captured and booked." Most ad spend buys clicks, not customers. We build the whole path — targeting, offer, landing page and follow-up — around your best-fit buyers, then let AI handle qualification and nurturing so your team only talks to people ready to buy. Channels are a means; booked calls are the goal.
Sub-services: Meta Ads; Google PPC; LinkedIn Ads; SEO; Landing Pages & Funnels; Lead Magnets; Retargeting; CRO (conversion-rate optimization).

## Websites — /websites
"A website that earns its keep." A good-looking site that doesn't convert is a cost, not an asset. We start from how visitors actually decide, then build a fast, clear, trustworthy site that guides them to act — speed, clarity and conversion designed in from the first wireframe, not bolted on later.
Sub-services: Business Websites; Landing Pages; Web Apps; E-commerce Stores; Webflow / Custom Build; Core Web Vitals & Speed; CMS Integration.

## Custom Web & Mobile Apps — /custom-apps
"Software shaped to how you actually work." Off-the-shelf tools force your business to bend around them. We design around your real workflow and users, ship in focused milestones, and integrate with the systems you already run — AI-assisted where it adds leverage, with clean code you own and can extend.
Sub-services: iOS & Android Apps; Progressive Web Apps (PWA); SaaS Platforms; Customer Portals; API & Third-Party Integrations; UI/UX Design.

## ERPs — /erps
"Run your operation from one source of truth." When stock, finance and orders live in separate spreadsheets, decisions get slow and costly. We map how your business really runs, connect or build the modules you need, and automate the hand-offs in between — so your team stops fighting spreadsheets and starts trusting the numbers.
Sub-services: Inventory & Supply Chain; Finance & Accounting Modules; HR & Payroll; CRM Integration; Workflow Automation; Custom ERP Build.

## Content & Branding — /content-branding
"Be the obvious, trusted choice." In a crowded market, buyers choose the brand they trust. We define a clear brand and a content engine that reinforces it everywhere a buyer looks — so trust builds over time and your marketing works harder for the same spend.
Sub-services: Brand Identity & Logo; Brand Strategy; Social Media Content; Video & Reels; Copywriting; SEO Content; Email Marketing.

## Business Intelligence Dashboards — /custom-dashboards
"See your whole business at a glance." Most teams drown in data but starve for insight. We connect your sources, model the data once, and build live dashboards around the decisions you actually make — so the right number is one glance away, not a week of spreadsheet wrangling.
Sub-services: Real-Time KPI Dashboards; Data Warehousing; Power BI / Looker / Tableau; Sales & Marketing Analytics; Financial Reporting; Predictive Analytics.

## Digital Growth Consulting — /business-consulting
"A clear plan to grow — and the system to run it." Growth stalls when strategy and execution are disconnected. We diagnose your funnel, sales process and attribution, set a focused growth roadmap, and — because we also build — help put the systems in place to execute it. Advice you can actually run, not a report that gathers dust.
Sub-services: Go-To-Market Strategy; Sales Process & CRM; Funnel & Conversion Audit; Marketing Strategy; Growth & Scaling Roadmap; Analytics & Attribution.
