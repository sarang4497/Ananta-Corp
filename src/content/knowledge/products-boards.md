# Plywood, MDF & boards — products, specs, uses

## Plywood (brand: Duroply) — /products/plywood

### Duroply MR Moisture Resistant Plywood — /products/plywood/moisture-resistant/duroply-mr-moisture-resistant-plywood
- Interior plywood bonded with urea formaldehyde resin; **IS 303** (as stated by Duroply).
- For furniture, wardrobes, bed frames, shelving, wall partitions — normal
  indoor conditions. NOT for direct water exposure.
- Sheets 8×4 ft; thickness 4–25 mm (stocked: 6, 8, 12, 16, 18 mm).

### Duroply BWP Boiling Water Proof Plywood — /products/plywood/boiling-water-proof/duroply-bwp-boiling-water-proof-plywood
- Boiling water proof plywood, phenol formaldehyde resin; **IS 710** (as stated by Duroply).
- For kitchens (especially sink cabinets), bathroom vanities, exterior furniture.
- Sheets 8×4 ft; thickness 4–25 mm (stocked: 9, 12, 19 mm); custom on bulk.

**MR vs BWP:** BWP withstands boiling water/wet areas (phenol formaldehyde); MR
is for indoor moisture only (urea formaldehyde). Kitchen sink cabinets → BWP.

## MDF (brand: Action TESA) — /products/mdf

### Interior Grade MDF — /products/mdf/interior-grade/action-tesa-interior-grade-mdf
- Action TESA states its MDF is built with German technology, homogeneous
  (no layers), and complies with IS 12406. Do NOT claim CARB/E1/E2 for this product.
- Smooth surface for paint, veneer, laminate; furniture, cabinets, wall panels.
- 8×4 ft; thicknesses 1.9–25 mm.

### Exterior Grade MDF — /products/mdf/exterior-grade/action-tesa-exterior-grade-mdf
- Moisture-resistant (MR) grade for covered, humid and semi-outdoor areas:
  covered balconies, bathrooms, exterior wall panelling. NOT for direct rain or
  standing water. Do NOT claim CARB or "weather resistant".
- Fully exposed outdoor use needs an additional protective coating.
- 8×4 ft; thicknesses 3.3–25 mm.

### HDHMR Board — /products/mdf/hdhmr/action-tesa-hdhmr-board
- High Density High Moisture Resistant: density over **850 kg/m³** and German
  mat-forming technology (as stated by Action TESA); good screw-holding.
  Do NOT state any warranty period (unconfirmed).
- The preferred board for modular kitchen carcasses, bathroom vanities,
  commercial interiors, heavy-duty furniture.
- 8×4 ft; thicknesses listed: 3, 5.5, 7.5, 11, 16.75, 18, 25 mm.

**MDF vs plywood:** MDF = compressed fibres, smooth uniform surface, best for
decorative/painted work. Plywood = layered veneers, stronger for load-bearing.

## Moist Master HMR Board (Action TESA) — /products/high-moisture-resistant-board-moist-master
- /products/high-moisture-resistant-board-moist-master/moist-master/action-tesa-moist-master-hmr-board
- High moisture resistant particle board, density above 750 kg/m³; termite &
  borer resistant (as stated by Action TESA); smooth laminate-ready face.
- For modular kitchen carcasses, bathroom furniture, wardrobe interiors.
- Thicknesses 9 / 12 / 18 mm, 8×4 ft.
- **HMR vs HDHMR:** HDHMR is denser (better screw-holding, structural rigidity);
  HMR is the economical standard-density option.

HMR** (smooth, moisture-resistant carcass boards). Sink cabinets and
direct-water zones → BWP plywood. Explain the trade-off, then route to WhatsApp
