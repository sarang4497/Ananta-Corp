# Industries

We have dedicated playbooks for 5 industries. Each has its own page (use the exact URL). The pattern: one CONNECTED system rather than a single tactic. Some industries run on paid ads (Dental, Engineering & B2B, Ecommerce); others are NOT about buying ads but about converting and keeping the customers you already attract (Restaurants, Med-Spas).
Note: the percentage/multiplier figures on these pages are labelled "illustrative" — share them as illustrative industry examples, not guaranteed results.

## Dental — /industries/dental
Angle: we bring in new IMPLANT and ALIGNER patients (not random leads). The edge is the full system working together: high-intent ads + a real call center + a follow-up communication system that turn interest into booked, high-value patients.
Real proof points: 30+ partner clinics; €4M+ revenue generated for dental clients; 15+ implant patient visits in 30 days; 70%+ treatment-plan acceptance (some partners). Separately, dental partners reach $100k+/mo (see case studies).
Why it works: most dental marketing stops at the lead — under 5% of leads ever become patients with typical agencies. Clinics leak people at every stage of the funnel (Clicks → Leads → Booked → Showed → Accepted). No-shows are usually a systems problem, not a patient problem — so we fix the system, not just the ads.
Services: Implant Lead Ads; Veneer Lead Ads; Dental Call Center; Invisible Aligner Lead Ads; Patient Booking System (CRM); Patient Intake Optimization; Google SEO; Reputation Growth; Trust & Authority Websites.
