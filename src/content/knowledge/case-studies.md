## Flagship results
- **Dental Practice Growth** — scaled 30+ practices to $100k+/month using creative ad campaigns, a dedicated call center, and CRM workflows from first click to a booked chair. (See also the Dental industry page: 30+ partners, €4M+ generated, 15+ implant visits in 30 days.)
- **Lubi Electronics — AI-Assisted Hiring System** — a major hiring bottleneck (10,000+ résumés unsorted across inboxes, ~10 arriving daily) turned into one AI hiring engine: all 10,000+ résumés centralized into a searchable dashboard, every candidate given an AI fit score, the team can simply CHAT with the dashboard to fill a role (it auto-sends AI interview links to fit candidates), and it auto-shortlists from those AI interviews so final interviews are only with the strongest, pre-vetted candidates. We also built their website (lubielectronics.com). Strong showcase for our AI-Assisted Hiring product.
- **Nisarg Nutritions** — 8× ROAS across India; scaled the Shopify store nationwide with structured Meta and Google campaigns tuned for return.
- **HSTUS** — 5× ROAS for a toys brand; built the Shopify store (hstus.com) and ran Google Shopping campaigns tuned for ROAS.
