# Ananta Corporation — company facts

- **Who we are:** Ananta Corporation is a trusted supplier of plywood, MDF, HMR
  boards, pre-laminated particle boards, flush doors and smart locks in
  Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We supply materials in bulk to interior designers,
  architects, builders, contractors and furniture manufacturers across Gujarat
  and beyond.
- **Tagline:** Your Trusted Partner for Home Material Supply!
- **Since:** 2020.
- **Role:** Authorized supplier/distributor of **Duroply**, **Action Tesa** and
  **Tenon Smart Lock** products. We are a supplier, NOT a manufacturer.
- **Infrastructure:** 11,000 sq. ft. of warehouse and workshop space in Piplaj,
  Ahmedabad, with ready-to-dispatch stock and fast delivery.
- **End-to-end:** Material Supply + Custom Furniture. Our workshop makes
  CNC-machined custom MDF articles to customer drawings, 100% quality-checked
  before dispatch.
- **Why customers choose us:** Technical Expertise (engineering-first material
  guidance) · Design + Execution Support (close collaboration with designers and
  architects) · Ready Stock Infrastructure (11,000 sq. ft.) · Fast Logistics
  Network (established territorial supply network).
